My Bookshelf Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 80EZ Studio handles information when you use the My Bookshelf Android application.

My Bookshelf is a personal reading tracker that allows you to organise books, track reading progress, save notes and quotes, record ratings, and manage your reading activity. The app can be used in local-only mode or with an optional account and cloud backup.

1. Who We Are

My Bookshelf is developed and published by 80EZ Studio.

Contact: 80ezstudio@gmail.com

2. Local-Only Mode

My Bookshelf can be used in local-only mode without creating or signing into an account.

In local-only mode, your books, notes, quotes, ratings, reading progress, preferences, and other reading information are stored on your device.

Local-only data is not uploaded to your My Bookshelf cloud account. 80EZ Studio cannot remotely access or delete information that exists only on your device.

You can remove local-only data by deleting it inside the app, clearing the app's storage through your device settings, or uninstalling the app. Android or device backup systems may retain a backup according to your device and Google account settings.

3. Information You Provide or Create

Depending on the features you use, My Bookshelf may process the following information:

  • Books added to your bookshelf
  • Book titles, authors, descriptions, covers, ISBNs and page counts
  • Reading status and reading progress
  • Reading start and completion dates
  • Ratings and favourites
  • Notes, quotes and other text you choose to save
  • Profile preferences, display name, reading bio and preset avatar
  • App settings and preferences
  • Email address and account identifier when you create an account
  • Authentication provider, such as email/password or Google Sign-In
  • Cloud backup and restore information

4. Account and Authentication Information

My Bookshelf uses Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google, for:

  • Email and password account creation
  • Email and password sign-in
  • Google Sign-In
  • Password reset
  • Authentication and account security
  • Reauthentication before sensitive actions
  • Account deletion

When you create or use an account, Firebase Authentication may process information such as your email address, Firebase user identifier, authentication provider, authentication tokens and related technical information needed to provide and secure the service.

Passwords and Google authentication credentials are handled through Firebase Authentication and Google authentication services. 80EZ Studio does not receive or store your full Google password.

5. Cloud Backup and Restore

If you sign in and use cloud backup or restore, your account library data is uploaded to and restored from Firebase Firestore.

Cloud-backed information may include:

  • Books in your signed-in account library
  • Book identifiers and related metadata
  • Notes and saved quotes
  • Ratings and favourites
  • Reading progress
  • Reading statuses
  • Dates and synchronisation information

Cloud information is associated with the Firebase user identifier for the signed-in account. Cloud backup and restore are optional because My Bookshelf can also be used in local-only mode.

6. Firebase and Google Services

My Bookshelf uses Firebase Authentication, Firebase Firestore and Firebase Cloud Functions.

These services are used for account management, authentication, cloud backup, cloud restore, account deletion, security and related app functionality.

Firebase and Google may process technical information needed to operate and secure their services. Their processing is governed by their own privacy and security policies.

7. Account and Data Deletion

If you create a My Bookshelf account, you can permanently delete it directly inside the app by opening: Settings > Account > Delete account.

You may be required to reauthenticate using your password or Google account before deletion can continue.

When account deletion succeeds, My Bookshelf deletes:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • Your cloud-backed books
  • Your cloud-backed notes and quotes
  • Your ratings and favourites
  • Your reading progress
  • Your reading statuses
  • Related book metadata stored with your cloud account
  • Local app data on that device belonging to the signed-in account

Books and notes saved in local-only mode are not associated with your cloud account and are not deleted when you delete an account.

Successful in-app deletion is normally completed immediately. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion through our public account-deletion page:

https://80ezstudio.github.io/MyBookshelf/delete-account/

Support-assisted deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days after the request has been verified.

8. Book Search and Book Information

My Bookshelf uses external book-information services to provide search results and book details.

These services may include Google Books and Open Library.

When you perform a search, information such as a book title, author, keyword or ISBN may be transmitted to the relevant provider so that search results can be returned.

External book-information providers operate under their own privacy policies. 80EZ Studio does not control their data-retention or processing practices.

9. Barcode Scanning and Camera Permission

My Bookshelf may request access to your device camera so that you can scan a book barcode or ISBN.

Camera access is used only when you choose to use the scanning feature. My Bookshelf does not upload or store photographs or video captured by the barcode scanner.

You can grant, deny or revoke camera permission through your device settings.

10. Amazon Affiliate Links

My Bookshelf may provide links to Amazon book pages.

Some of these links are affiliate links. If you follow an affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase, 80EZ Studio may receive a commission at no additional cost to you.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

When you select an Amazon link, you leave My Bookshelf and interact directly with Amazon's website or application. Amazon's privacy policy, terms, payment processes, delivery procedures and account practices apply to that interaction.

My Bookshelf does not collect or store your Amazon password, payment card details, delivery address or Amazon purchase history.

11. Analytics, Advertising and Crash Reporting

My Bookshelf does not currently use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, personalised advertising or third-party advertising SDKs.

My Bookshelf does not sell your personal information or your saved reading information.

Limited technical logs may be produced by the app, Android, Firebase or other service providers when needed to operate, secure or diagnose their services.

If analytics, crash-reporting tools or advertising services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety declarations will be reviewed and updated before those services are included in a released version where required.

12. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Provide and operate My Bookshelf
  • Save and display your personal bookshelf
  • Track reading progress and reading status
  • Store notes, quotes, ratings and favourites
  • Provide book search and barcode-scanning features
  • Create, authenticate and manage user accounts
  • Provide cloud backup and restore
  • Process account deletion
  • Protect accounts and prevent misuse
  • Respond to support requests
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of the app

13. Data Sharing and Service Providers

80EZ Studio does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by trusted service providers where necessary to operate My Bookshelf. These providers include:

  • Google Firebase for authentication, cloud storage and Cloud Functions
  • Google Books for book search and book information
  • Open Library for book search and book information
  • Amazon when you choose to open an Amazon or affiliate link
  • Google Play and Android services used to distribute and operate the app

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

14. Data Retention

Local-only information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to any Android or device backup settings.

Cloud-backed information is retained while your account and cloud library remain active, or until you delete specific information or delete your account.

Successful account deletion normally removes the Firebase Authentication account and associated My Bookshelf cloud-backed data immediately.

Firebase, Google or other service providers may retain limited technical, security, fraud-prevention or legal records under their own retention policies or where required by law.

15. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information handled through My Bookshelf.

Information transmitted to Firebase, Google Books, Open Library and other online services is sent using encrypted network connections where supported by those services.

No method of electronic transmission, processing or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for protecting your device, login credentials and Google account.

16. Children's Privacy

My Bookshelf is a general-audience application and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

80EZ Studio does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through My Bookshelf, contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

17. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing.

You can also:

  • Use My Bookshelf without an account in local-only mode
  • Choose whether to use cloud backup and restore
  • Delete books, notes and other information inside the app
  • Delete your account through the app
  • Manage camera permission through device settings
  • Choose whether to open external or affiliate links
  • Clear app storage or uninstall the app

To make a privacy or data request, email: 80ezstudio@gmail.com

18. International Processing

Google, Firebase and other service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

Where applicable, these providers use legal and security safeguards for international data processing under their own policies and contractual arrangements.

19. Future Features
