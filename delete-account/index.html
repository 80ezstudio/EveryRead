Delete your My Bookshelf account

This page explains how to permanently delete your My Bookshelf account and the cloud data associated with it.

Delete your account in the app

You can permanently delete your account directly in My Bookshelf by opening: Settings > Account > Delete account.

You may be asked to reauthenticate using your password or Google account. Successful in-app deletion is normally completed immediately.

If you cannot access the app

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us:

Request account deletion

Please send the request from the email address linked to your My Bookshelf account where possible. If that is not possible, include the account email address in your message so we can verify the request.

What data will be deleted

When deletion succeeds, we permanently delete your My Bookshelf Firebase Authentication account and the cloud-backed data associated with that account.

This may include:

  • Cloud-backed books
  • Notes and saved quotes
  • Ratings
  • Reading progress
  • Reading statuses
  • Related book metadata stored with your account

Data stored locally on the device under your signed-in account is also removed by the app after successful account deletion.

Local-only data

Books and notes saved in local-only mode are stored only on your device and are not associated with your cloud account. Account deletion does not delete this local-only data.

You can remove local-only data by deleting it inside the app, clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the app. Android or device backup systems may retain backups according to your device and account settings.

What data may be retained

We may retain limited technical or legal records where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or operational reasons. Firebase and Google may also retain limited records under their own policies.

Any retained information will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Processing time

Successful in-app deletion is normally completed immediately. Support-assisted deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days after we verify the request.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
