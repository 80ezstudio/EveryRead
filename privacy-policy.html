My Bookshelf Privacy Policy

Version: 2026.07.12

This Privacy Policy explains how 80EZ Studio collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information when you use My Bookshelf.

My Bookshelf is a personal reading tracker. It can be used in local-only mode without an account and also provides optional account, cloud, subscription, book-discovery and social features where available.

80EZ Studio is responsible for the personal information processed through My Bookshelf. Contact: 80ezstudio@gmail.com.

Public copy: https://80ezstudio.github.io/MyBookshelf/privacy-policy.html

1. Scope

This policy applies to the My Bookshelf Android app, its account and cloud services, My Bookshelf Pro, social features, support communications, and the related public legal and account-deletion pages.

Google Play, Google, Firebase, RevenueCat, Amazon, book-data providers and other third parties may also process information under their own privacy notices.

2. Age requirements and parental responsibility

My Bookshelf is a 13+ account service. A person must be at least 13 years old to create an account or use social features.

A child under 13 may use the local-only reading tracker only where a parent or guardian controls the device and supervises that use. A child under 13 must not create an account, provide personal information to 80EZ Studio or use social features.

My Bookshelf may ask for birth month and birth year to determine an age band. The app is designed to retain the resulting age band rather than a full date of birth.

Users aged 13 to 15 may use permitted private reading and account features, but social features are unavailable unless a legally appropriate verified parent or guardian approval process is introduced.

Users aged 16 to 17 receive private-by-default settings. Discoverability is off by default, unrestricted stranger messaging is not permitted, and social interaction is limited to closed-network features made available by My Bookshelf.

If the law applying to a user requires a higher age or additional parent or guardian authorisation, the stricter requirement applies.

Parents and guardians are responsible for deciding whether the app is suitable for a minor, supervising use, protecting the device and account, authorising purchases and teaching the minor not to share personal or sensitive information. My Bookshelf does not replace active parental supervision.

3. Information we process

Reading and library information, including books, authors, ISBNs, covers, descriptions, page counts, reading status, progress, dates, ratings, favourites, history, goals, notes, quotes and app preferences.

Account and profile information, including email address, Firebase user identifier, sign-in provider, authentication status, username, display name, reading biography, preset avatar, visibility settings, age band and records of required Terms or safety acknowledgements.

Social information, including friend relationships, requests, invitations, Page Partner participants, shared progress, text messages, reactions and social notifications.

Safety information, including blocks, reports, reported content references, evidence snapshots, moderation decisions, warnings, restrictions, suspensions, bans, appeals and audit records.

Purchase information, including product and transaction identifiers, purchase history, trial and subscription status, entitlement status, renewal, expiry, refund or cancellation status, and an app user identifier used by RevenueCat.

Technical and support information, including app version, device and operating-system information, authentication and security events, error information, service timestamps, technical logs and information you provide when contacting support or making a request.

Camera permission may be requested for barcode or ISBN scanning. The scanner is not intended to upload or retain photographs or videos.

Do not put an address, school, phone number, personal email address, precise location, financial information, password or other sensitive information in a username, profile, invitation or message.

4. Local-only and cloud storage

In local-only mode, reading information is stored on your device and is not associated with a Firebase account by 80EZ Studio. We cannot remotely access, restore or delete information that exists only on your device.

You can remove local-only data by deleting it in the app, clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Android, Google or device backup systems may retain copies according to settings controlled by you and the relevant provider.

My Bookshelf uses Firebase Authentication for account access, Firebase Firestore for cloud-backed information and Firebase Cloud Functions for secure server-side operations.

Cloud-backed information may include library data, notes, quotes, profile information, social relationships, Page Partner activity, messages, blocks, reports, moderation records and account settings.

Cloud storage, synchronisation and restoration are conveniences. You remain responsible for keeping an independent copy of information that is important to you.

5. How and why we use information

To provide the features you request, operate accounts, store and restore cloud data, manage subscriptions, deliver permitted social features, provide support and process account deletion. The usual lawful basis is performance of our agreement with you.

To protect users and the service, apply age and privacy restrictions, enforce blocks, investigate reports, prevent fraud and abuse, moderate content, secure accounts and enforce our Terms. The usual lawful basis is our legitimate interests in safety, security, service integrity and protecting users.

To comply with legal obligations, lawful requests, consumer rules, data-protection requirements, online-safety duties, accounting obligations and court or regulatory requirements.

In a genuine emergency, to process or disclose limited information where legally permitted and reasonably necessary to protect life or prevent serious harm.

Where consent is legally required for a separate optional activity, we will request it separately. Agreeing to this Privacy Policy is not consent to every form of processing.

6. Social visibility, blocking and moderation

Private library information is not made public merely because you create an account.

Information is visible to another user only through a feature that permits it and subject to age, privacy, relationship, participation and block controls. This may include a username, display name, preset avatar, reading biography, selected reading activity, invitations, Page Partner messages and reactions.

You are responsible for considering what you choose to share. Another user may copy, screenshot or disclose information outside My Bookshelf despite the rules, and 80EZ Studio cannot guarantee another user's conduct or identity.

Where available, blocking is intended to prevent blocked accounts from finding or viewing one another, sending requests or invitations, sending Page Partner messages and generating related social notifications.

Reports may preserve relevant content, account identifiers, evidence snapshots, timestamps, decisions and audit records. We may remove content, restrict social access, suspend or ban accounts, preserve evidence, prevent evasion or make a lawful disclosure where appropriate.

We do not promise that every message will be reviewed before delivery or that every harmful interaction will be detected immediately.

7. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell personal information and do not currently use it for personalised advertising.

We may share information, or allow it to be processed, only where reasonably necessary with Google Firebase and Google Play for authentication, cloud services, security, app distribution and billing; RevenueCat for purchase verification and subscription access; Google Books, Open Library and The New York Times for book information and recommendations; Amazon when you open an affiliate link; and hosting, email, security, support or moderation providers used to operate the service.

We may also disclose limited information to regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies, emergency services, app stores or other parties where required by law or reasonably and lawfully necessary to protect users, rights or security.

Other users receive only the information made available through the feature you choose and subject to applicable access controls.

8. Affiliate links and external services

My Bookshelf may contain Amazon affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Opening an affiliate link may take you to Amazon or another third-party service. That third party is responsible for its accounts, cookies, payments, purchases, delivery, returns and privacy practices.

My Bookshelf does not receive your full Amazon payment-card details, Amazon password, delivery address or complete Amazon purchase history.

9. International processing

Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where a restricted international transfer applies, we rely on an available lawful safeguard, such as UK adequacy regulations, approved contractual safeguards or another permitted transfer mechanism.

10. Retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

Local-only information remains on your device until you remove it, subject to device backups. Active account and cloud information is normally retained while the account remains active and the relevant feature is used.

Support, complaint, appeal, purchase and entitlement records are retained as needed to resolve the matter, verify access, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

Reports, evidence snapshots, blocks, warnings, suspensions, bans and audit records may be retained after content or account deletion where reasonably necessary for safety, abuse prevention, legal obligations, appeals, disputes or legal claims.

You can request account deletion in the app at Settings > Account > Delete account or at https://80ezstudio.github.io/MyBookshelf/delete-account/. You may need to reauthenticate.

Successful deletion removes or initiates deletion of the Firebase Authentication account and My Bookshelf cloud information associated with it, subject to limited lawful retention for safety, moderation, evidence, security, fraud prevention, purchase verification, billing disputes, appeals, legal obligations and provider backup cycles.

Information stored only in local-only mode must be removed separately from the device. Deleting the app or your My Bookshelf account does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

Support-assisted deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days after account ownership has been sufficiently verified.

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to access personal information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion or restriction, object to certain processing, receive eligible information in a portable format, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and request appropriate review of certain automated decisions.

These rights are not absolute. Information may be retained where required for safety, security, legal obligations, fraud prevention, the rights of others or legal claims.

To make a request, email 80ezstudio@gmail.com. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify identity, authority and account ownership.

You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

We will acknowledge a data-protection complaint within 30 days and respond without undue delay.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including authenticated access, backend access controls, encrypted network connections where supported, rate limits, audit records and reauthentication for sensitive actions.

No system is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device, Google account and My Bookshelf credentials, preventing account sharing, using appropriate device security and promptly reporting suspected unauthorised access.
