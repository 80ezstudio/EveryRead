My Bookshelf Privacy Policy

Updated on: 15 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 80EZ Studio collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use My Bookshelf.

My Bookshelf is a personal reading tracker designed to help you save books, track reading progress, manage notes and quotes, organise your personal bookshelf, and, where available and permitted, use account, cloud, book discovery, paid, subscription, affiliate, safety, and social features.

Who We Are

My Bookshelf is developed and published by 80EZ Studio and may be made available in the United Kingdom, the United States, and other countries. For personal information processed through My Bookshelf, 80EZ Studio is the data controller or business responsible for the information unless a third-party service acts as an independent controller or business under its own privacy terms.

Contact email: 80ezstudio@gmail.com

Important Age Rules

My Bookshelf Pro and Community are currently available only to users aged 18 or over.

Users under 18 may continue to use the Free features that are available to them, including local-only reading tracker features. An under-18 result blocks access to Pro and Community even if a subscription or entitlement was previously associated with the account.

My Bookshelf does not ask Free users for a birth date merely to use the Free reading tracker. An age assessment is requested only when a user deliberately attempts to access Pro, Community, or another feature that requires adult eligibility.

When an age assessment is required, the birth date you enter is processed only to determine an age band. My Bookshelf stores the resulting age band, the date and time of the assessment, and the age-assessment policy version used. The full birth date is not retained in the My Bookshelf database, user profile, or application logs.

The current age bands are under 13, 13 to 15, 16 to 17, and adult. Only the adult age band is eligible for Pro and Community. Users assessed as under 18 are not shown Pro prices, trial offers, purchase controls, or restore-purchase controls, and the app does not intentionally begin a purchase request for them.

Account, Login, Email Verification, and Firebase Services

My Bookshelf uses Firebase services provided by Google to support account creation, email-and-password login, Google Sign-In, password reset, email verification, reauthentication, account security, cloud backup, cloud restore, social features, safety features, and account deletion.

Firebase Authentication is used to create and manage accounts and verify signed-in users. Firebase Cloud Functions support secure server-side operations, including access checks, social actions, safety actions, and account deletion.

Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity may be used to help confirm that requests come from an authentic app installation and to prevent fraud, automated abuse, unauthorised access, or misuse of backend services.

If you create or use an account, Firebase or related service providers may process your email address, user identifier, authentication provider, authentication tokens, email-verification status, device or app information, integrity information, and technical logs.

A verified email may be required before cloud backup, Pro purchasing, Community access, or other account-based features can be used. Email verification is one access requirement and does not by itself make a user eligible for Pro or Community.

Firebase and Google services are provided by Google and may process information in accordance with their own privacy and security practices.

Local-Only Mode

My Bookshelf can be used in local-only mode without creating or signing into an account. In local-only mode, your books, notes, quotes, ratings, reading progress, reading status, preferences, and other reading information are stored on your device.

Local-only data is not uploaded to your My Bookshelf cloud account and is not associated with a Firebase user account. 80EZ Studio cannot remotely access or delete information that exists only on your device.

You can remove local-only data by deleting it inside the app, clearing the app's storage through your device settings, or uninstalling the app. Android, Google, or device backup systems may retain copies according to your device and account backup settings.

Local and Cloud Storage

Some My Bookshelf data is stored locally on your device. This may include your books, reading progress, notes, quotes, preferences, ratings, cached book information, and other app information.

If you sign in and use cloud backup, restore, profile, Pro, Community, safety, or subscription features, information may also be uploaded to and restored from Firebase Firestore or processed by related service providers.

Cloud-backed information may include:

  • Books associated with your signed-in account.
  • Book titles, authors, identifiers, covers, descriptions, and related book metadata.
  • Reading progress and reading status.
  • Ratings and favourites.
  • Notes and saved quotes.
  • Reading dates, goals, and synchronisation information.
  • Usernames, profile information, visibility settings, friend relationships, friend requests, Community activity, Page Partner records, messages, invitations, reactions, and notifications.
  • Age-band and age-assessment records used to enforce adult-only access.
  • Safety records such as blocks, reports, evidence snapshots, moderation records, audit logs, restrictions, suspensions, bans, and appeals.

Cloud-backed information is associated with the user identifier for your signed-in Firebase account. Cloud backup and restore are optional because My Bookshelf can also be used in local-only mode.

Adult-Only Pro and Community Access

Pro and Community are currently restricted to adults aged 18 or over. Community includes social and user-interaction features such as public or discoverable profiles, friends, friend requests, shared reading activity, Page Partners, invitations, participant lists, messages, reactions, notifications, blocking, reporting, and moderation.

When you deliberately attempt to access Pro or Community, My Bookshelf may require you to sign in, complete an age assessment, verify your email, and satisfy the current Pro entitlement requirements. These checks are used only to decide whether the requested adult-only feature can be made available.

If the result is under 18, access is refused and the user may continue using available Free features. The age-assessment result takes priority over a purchase or entitlement record. The age assessment is not used for advertising, behavioural profiling, or personalising book recommendations.

We may ask for a new assessment if the stored assessment is missing, becomes outdated, or the age-assessment rules materially change.

Profiles, Username Discoverability, Friends, and Community Activity

Community is currently available only to eligible adults. If you use Community, profile information may be visible to other eligible users depending on your settings and the feature being used. This may include your username, reading bio, selected preset avatar, public profile fields, reading activity you choose to share, and whether your profile can be discovered through username search.

Page Partner features may process invitations, accepted or declined invitation records, participant lists, related book information, progress, page-linked messages, reactions, notifications, and safety reports. Page Partner messages and invitations may be visible to the people involved in that Page Partner activity and to authorised reviewers where required for safety, support, or moderation.

Deleting an item or account may therefore remove it from active My Bookshelf systems without removing copies previously made by another person.

Blocking, Reporting, Moderation, and Safety Records

My Bookshelf may provide blocking, reporting, moderation, warning, restriction, suspension, ban, and appeal features to protect users and maintain safe Community spaces.

Account and Data Deletion

If you create a My Bookshelf account, you can permanently delete it directly inside the app by opening: Settings > Account > Delete account.

You may need to reauthenticate using your password or Google account before deletion can continue.

When account deletion succeeds, My Bookshelf begins deletion of:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account.
  • Your cloud-backed books.
  • Your cloud-backed notes and saved quotes.
  • Your ratings and favourites.
  • Your reading progress and reading statuses.
  • Related book metadata stored with your cloud account.
  • Cloud-backed profile and social records associated with your account, except for limited records retained for safety, security, legal, audit, dispute, fraud-prevention, or abuse-prevention purposes.
  • Local app data on that device that belongs to the signed-in account where the app can remove it.

Books, notes, and other reading information stored only in local-only mode are not associated with your cloud account and are not deleted when you delete a cloud account.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Active account data is normally removed promptly, but limited records and provider-held backups or technical records may remain for the retention purposes described in this policy.

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion of your My Bookshelf account and associated account data using the account-deletion page: https://80ezstudio.github.io/MyBookshelf/delete-account/

Verified support-assisted deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days. We may ask for information needed to confirm that you control the account before acting on the request.

Firebase, Google, RevenueCat, Google Play, or other service providers may retain limited technical, security, fraud-prevention, operational, purchase, tax, accounting, or legal records under their own retention policies or where required by law.

Automated Age Assessment and Access Decisions

My Bookshelf uses the birth date entered during the age-assessment flow to calculate an age band. The logic compares the date with the current date and assigns the user to an age band. The birth date is then discarded rather than stored as part of the account.

The result is used to decide whether Pro and Community may be accessed. An adult result allows the user to continue to the remaining eligibility checks. An under-18 result blocks Pro and Community and allows the user to return to available Free features.

This decision is not used for advertising, credit, employment, insurance, or behavioural profiling. If you believe an age result is incorrect or want a decision reviewed, contact 80ezstudio@gmail.com.
