My Bookshelf Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 80EZ Studio handles information when you use My Bookshelf.

My Bookshelf is a personal reading tracker app. It is designed to help you save books, track reading progress, manage notes and quotes, organise your personal bookshelf, and, where available, use account, login, sync, backup, paid, subscription, affiliate, or book discovery features.

Who We Are

My Bookshelf is developed and published by 80EZ Studio.

Contact email: 80ezstudio@gmail.com

Information We Collect

My Bookshelf may collect, store, or process information you provide or create in the app so that the app can operate and provide its features.

This may include:

  • Books you add to your bookshelf
  • Book titles, authors, page counts, cover information, descriptions, ISBNs, and related book details
  • Reading status, reading progress, ratings, reading dates, favourites, and reading history
  • Notes, quotes, and other reading-related text you choose to save
  • Profile preferences, such as display name, reading bio, and selected preset avatar
  • App settings and preferences
  • Account-related information, such as email address, user ID, login provider, authentication status, and account settings
  • Cloud backup and restore information associated with your signed-in account
  • Purchase or entitlement information, where paid features are available, such as whether your account or device has access to a paid feature, subscription, trial, or paid entitlement
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the app, such as app version, device information, crash or error information, and service logs where these features are enabled
  • Affiliate link interaction information, only if link tracking or analytics are enabled, such as whether an affiliate link was tapped

Account, Login, and Firebase Services

My Bookshelf uses Firebase services provided by Google to support account creation, email and password login, Google Sign-In, password reset, reauthentication, account security, cloud backup, cloud restore, and account deletion.

Firebase Authentication is used to create and manage accounts and verify signed-in users.

Firebase Firestore is used to store cloud-backed library data for signed-in users who choose to use cloud backup or restore.

Firebase Cloud Functions are used to support secure account deletion and related server-side operations.

If you create or use an account, Firebase or related service providers may process account and technical information needed to provide the service. This may include your email address, user identifier, authentication provider, authentication tokens, device or app information, and technical logs.

Firebase and Google services are provided by Google and may process information in accordance with their own privacy and security practices. We use these services to operate My Bookshelf features, manage accounts, maintain security, provide cloud backup and restore, and support app functionality.

Local-Only Mode

My Bookshelf can be used in local-only mode without creating or signing into an account.

In local-only mode, your books, notes, quotes, ratings, reading progress, reading status, preferences, and other reading information are stored on your device.

Local-only data is not uploaded to your My Bookshelf cloud account and is not associated with a Firebase user account.

80EZ Studio cannot remotely access or delete information that exists only on your device.

You can remove local-only data by deleting it inside the app, clearing the app's storage through your device settings, or uninstalling the app. Android, Google, or device backup systems may retain backups according to your device and account backup settings.

Local and Cloud Storage

Some My Bookshelf data is stored locally on your device. This may include your books, reading progress, notes, quotes, preferences, ratings, and other app information.

If you sign in and use cloud backup or restore, account library information may also be uploaded to and restored from Firebase Firestore.

Cloud-backed information may include:

  • Books associated with your signed-in account
  • Book titles, authors, identifiers, covers, descriptions, and related book metadata
  • Reading progress and reading status
  • Ratings and favourites
  • Notes and saved quotes
  • Reading dates and synchronisation information

Cloud-backed information is associated with the user identifier for your signed-in Firebase account.

Cloud backup and restore are optional because My Bookshelf can also be used in local-only mode.

This means that not all app data is stored only on your device once account, cloud, sync, backup, or paid features are enabled.

Account and Data Deletion

If you create a My Bookshelf account, you can permanently delete it directly inside the app by opening: Settings > Account > Delete account.

You may need to reauthenticate using your password or Google account before deletion can continue.

When account deletion succeeds, My Bookshelf deletes:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • Your cloud-backed books
  • Your cloud-backed notes and saved quotes
  • Your ratings and favourites
  • Your reading progress
  • Your reading statuses
  • Related book metadata stored with your cloud account
  • Local app data on that device that belongs to the signed-in account

Books, notes, and other reading information stored only in local-only mode are not associated with your cloud account and are not deleted when you delete an account.

Successful in-app deletion is normally completed immediately.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion of your My Bookshelf account and associated account data using this page:

https://80ezstudio.github.io/MyBookshelf/delete-account/

Support-assisted deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days after the request has been verified.

Firebase, Google, or other service providers may retain limited technical, security, fraud-prevention, operational, or legal records under their own retention policies or where required by law.

Book Search and Book Information

My Bookshelf may use external book information sources to help search for books and display book details such as title, author, description, cover image, page count, and identifiers such as ISBN.

These services may include Google Books and Open Library.

When you search for a book, the search terms, author, keyword, ISBN, or related search information you enter may be sent to the relevant book information service so search results can be returned.

These services are not controlled by 80EZ Studio and may process data according to their own privacy policies.

My Bookshelf does not sell your saved reading notes, personal bookshelf, ratings, or reading progress.

Affiliate Links

My Bookshelf may include links to third-party websites or services, including Amazon book pages.

Some links may be affiliate links. This means that if you tap a link and make a qualifying purchase, 80EZ Studio may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

When you tap an affiliate link, you may leave My Bookshelf and visit a third-party website, app, or service, such as Amazon. We do not control those third-party websites or services, and their own privacy policies, terms, payment processes, delivery processes, returns policies, and data practices will apply.

My Bookshelf does not collect your Amazon payment details, card details, delivery address, Amazon account password, or Amazon purchase history. Any purchase, payment, delivery, return, refund, or account activity on Amazon is handled by Amazon.

We may record that an affiliate link was tapped only if analytics or link tracking are enabled in the app. If this is enabled, the information may be used to understand which links are used, improve book discovery features, and support app functionality.

My Bookshelf may offer paid features, subscriptions, one-time purchases, paid access, trials, or other paid products now or in the future.

Payments and purchase management may be handled by Google Play or another authorised payment provider. We do not directly receive or store your full payment card details.

We may receive or process information needed to confirm, unlock, manage, or restore paid access. This may include purchase status, subscription status, entitlement status, product identifiers, transaction identifiers, account identifiers, and related technical information.

Paid features, prices, subscription options, included features, trial availability, billing periods, and access rules may change over time, subject to applicable law and app store rules.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally on your device remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to Android, Google, or device backup settings.

Successful in-app account deletion normally removes the Firebase Authentication account and associated My Bookshelf cloud-backed data immediately.

You may delete local app data by deleting information within the app where controls are available, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app.

Local-only data is not deleted as part of cloud-account deletion because it is not associated with the cloud account.

You can request deletion of your My Bookshelf account and associated account data using this page: https://80ezstudio.github.io/MyBookshelf/delete-account/

Firebase, Google, or other service providers may retain limited technical, legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational records under their own policies or where required by law.
